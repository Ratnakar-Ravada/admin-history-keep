At a glance. Location Keeper is a private dashboard for authorised device locations, journeys and activity reports. Google sign-in identifies your account. 1. Who we are and what this policy covers

Ratnakar Ravada operates Location Keeper at admin-location-keep.rockyworks.in. This policy covers the website and dashboard, Google sign-in, and the optional Gmail sender connection. For privacy questions or requests, email ratnakarravada11@gmail.com.

The dashboard displays reports supplied through a separately configured device tracking integration. Use it only for devices you own or have permission to manage. Visiting this website or signing in does not start device tracking or grant access to someone else's devices. Collection by a separately installed tracking app also depends on that app's permissions and settings.

2. Information we access and collect

  • Google account information: your Google account identifier, display name, email address, email verification status and, where applicable, Google Workspace domain. These identify your account and its access rights.
  • Device reports: device identifiers and names, recorded coordinates, timestamps, location accuracy, battery and network context, and reported location or permission events made available through the tracking integration. These reports are separate from Google account data; we do not obtain Google Location History through sign-in.
  • Sessions and usage: sign-in and activity times, browser and operating-system categories, device type, pages or views opened, foreground activity signals, API routes, response status, timing and response sizes. These records can be linked to your signed-in account and support security, troubleshooting and internal usage analytics.
  • Optional Gmail authorisation: the connected sender identity, connection time, Google OAuth access and refresh tokens, and the authorisation needed to send email. Alert delivery, when available and enabled, also processes configured recipient addresses and the alert subject, content and any selected attachments.
  • Communications and infrastructure data: information you provide when contacting us. Hosting and network providers also process connection details such as IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and protect the service.

3. Google permissions and their purposes

Ordinary sign-in requests only basic identity permissions. Gmail sending is requested separately when an eligible administrator explicitly chooses to connect the configured sender account.

Permission How Location Keeper uses it
openid Verify your Google identity and associate your permanent account identifier with the correct app account and session.
email Obtain your email address and verification status to check authorised account access and, for Gmail connection, confirm the sender account.
profile Use your display name to identify your account in the workspace and its internal account records. A profile photo is not required by the dashboard.
gmail.send
(optional)		 Send app alert emails from the Google account you connect to recipient email addresses you configure in Alerts. The full scope is https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.send.

We do not receive your Google password. Basic sign-in permissions do not authorise access to your Gmail inbox, contacts, calendar, Drive files or Google Location History. We do not use basic profile information for advertising or unrelated profiling.

4. Optional Gmail access and email alerts

The sole purpose of Gmail send permission is to send Location Keeper email alerts from the Google account you connect to the recipient email addresses you configure in the Alerts section. It is not used for marketing, unsolicited messages or unrelated communications. The permission does not authorise the app to read, search, modify or delete messages in your mailbox.

Current availability: sender connection is available to configured administrators. Automated alert sending and recipient configuration are still in development and are shown as coming soon. Connecting Gmail stores the sender authorisation; it does not itself send an email. The alert-purpose description above explains the intended use of the permission and does not mean automated alerts are already running.

When enabled, recipients receive the alert content you configure, which may include device names, events or location information. Choose recipients and content carefully and obtain any required permission to share that information. Google processes outgoing mail to deliver it, and recipients may retain their own copies.

Gmail access and refresh tokens are stored encrypted on the backend server, with restricted file permissions. Refresh tokens allow the connected sender authorisation to be renewed without asking you to sign in for each operation. Tokens are not placed in the public frontend or shared with map providers or alert recipients.

You can choose Alerts → Email delivery → Disconnect sender using the configured sender account to remove its locally stored authorisation. You can also revoke Location Keeper's access through Google Account connections. Disconnecting does not delete emails already delivered; revoking Google access does not itself erase app account or usage records.

5. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, enforce device and administrator permissions, display authorised reports, provide maps and place labels, maintain account sessions, understand app use, diagnose errors, protect the service and respond to support or privacy requests. The Gmail permission is limited to the alert purpose described above.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for targeted or personalised advertising, provide it to data brokers, or use it to train general-purpose AI or machine learning models. We do not use Google account information to determine creditworthiness or eligibility for lending.

6. When information is shared

  • Authorised viewers: device reports are available to accounts with the relevant permissions. Users granted analytics access can view account, session and usage records within the app.
  • Service providers: Google provides sign-in and optional Gmail delivery; Cloudflare provides website delivery, security and API tunnel routing; Google Cloud hosts the backend; and TiDB provides database storage. They process the data needed for their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.
  • Maps and place labels: Geoapify, or OpenFreeMap where configured, receives map requests. Geoapify can receive selected coordinates to resolve place labels. Browser map requests can expose your IP address and the area viewed to the map provider. We do not send Google authorisation tokens or Gmail message content to map providers.
  • Alert recipients: when alert delivery is available and enabled, messages go only to the addresses configured for those alerts.
  • Safety, legal requirements and changes of operator: information may be disclosed where necessary to investigate security incidents, comply with applicable law or protect legal rights. Any transfer of Google user data to a new operator is subject to Google's requirements, including prior consent where required.

Service providers may process data outside your country. Human access to Google-derived data is limited to the user-facing service you authorise, specific support access you agree to, necessary security or debugging work, legal obligations, or permitted aggregated internal operations. It is not used for unrelated inspection of your account.

7. Storage, security and cookies

Public website and API connections use HTTPS. Access controls restrict dashboard data, session cookies are encrypted and configured with HttpOnly and SameSite protections, and Gmail credentials are encrypted on the backend. These measures reduce risk; no system can guarantee complete security.

We use necessary cookies for sign-in, session management and short-lived OAuth connection flows. You can block or clear cookies in your browser, but doing so may prevent sign-in or end your session. We do not use Google user data for advertising cookies. Internal app usage records are separate from these necessary cookies.

8. Retention and deletion

Gmail sender credentials remain on the server until you disconnect the sender or the operator removes them. Expired or revoked Google grants cannot provide valid continued access; a local record can remain until it is removed. OAuth flow cookies are short-lived, and app sessions expire or can be ended by signing out.

Account, session-history, usage and tracking records currently have no automatic deletion cutoff and remain stored until the operator deletes them. Session expiry, signing out, revoking Google access and deleting an app account are separate actions. You can request deletion of your account and associated records using the privacy contact below. Deletion is handled by the operator, rather than an automated self-service account deletion tool.

We verify authority over a request, remove information that no longer needs to be kept, and explain any information retained for security, a legal obligation or another necessary service purpose. Device reports shared with other authorised users may require separate ownership checks. Removing records from this app does not delete copies held by email recipients, Google or the original tracking source. Any provider backup copies are subject to the provider's retention and deletion process.

9. Your choices and requests

You can use the public overview and this policy without a Google account. To view private reports, your account must be authorised. You can decline optional Gmail access, disconnect a sender, sign out, or revoke Google access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Contact us to request access to, correction of, export of or deletion of information associated with your account, or to raise a concern about device data access. We verify requests and handle them subject to applicable rights, the permissions of other users and any information we must retain. Do not send passwords, OAuth tokens or unnecessary location records in your initial request.

10. Google user data commitments

Location Keeper follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable. Information received through Google APIs is used and transferred only for the purposes disclosed in this policy and allowed by those requirements.

Location Keeper is an independent application operated by Ratnakar Ravada. It is not operated by or endorsed by Google. The service is not directed at children.
